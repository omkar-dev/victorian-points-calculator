Privacy

Privacy

VPT Points has no accounts and no sign-in. This page says what stays on your device, what is measured and which outside services the site uses.

What stays on your device

Calculator answers are worked out in your browser. They’re kept in the page address so you can bookmark or share a result, and they aren’t sent to VPT Points.

Journey tracker scenarios and saved occupations are stored in your browser’s local storage. They never leave your device unless you export a backup yourself. Clearing your browser data removes them.

What is measured

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and page load times. It doesn’t use cookies, doesn’t follow you across sites and doesn’t identify you. See Cloudflare Web Analytics.

Google Analytics 4 measures which pages are visited and how people find the site. It sets cookies. Page addresses are sent without their query string, so calculator answers aren’t included. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Other services

Pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare, which see standard request details such as your IP address to serve and protect the site. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

Questions about this page can be raised through the project on GitHub.
