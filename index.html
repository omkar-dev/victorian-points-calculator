      What changed

      Every birthday now counts

      Victoria scores Registrations of Interest more finely for 2026–27. Age is now scored for each year, and salary in $5,000 steps. Calculators that still use the old five-year age bands can be out by more than 100 points.

      Age

      Points for each year

      Ages 20–29 still get the full 300. After that you lose points every year, down to 136 at 40 and 16 at 44.

      Salary

      19 bands of $5,000

      Each $5,000 step adds about 8 points. You start at 8 points at $80,000 and reach the full 150 at $170,000.

      Fees

      $60 to register, $180 to apply

      Both fees are plus GST. You only pay the $180 for a 190 or 491 nomination application if Victoria invites you.

      How it works

      Seven factors, 950 points

      Age300+ English200+ Salary150+ Experience100+ Qualification100+ Skill level50+ Partner50= Total950

      Victoria takes these factors from your SkillSelect Expression of Interest and your Victorian Registration of Interest, then adds them up.

      Age and English make up more than half the total. A 29-year-old with Superior English is already on 500 points before anything else counts. If you already live and work in Victoria, salary is the factor you can change most easily. Each $5,000 band is worth 7 or 8 points.

      There is no pass mark. Victoria ranks everyone’s ROI against each other and invites the highest scorers in each occupation group, so the score you need depends on demand. You still need at least 65 points on the federal SkillSelect test to lodge your EOI.

      FAQ

      Questions people ask about the VPT

      What is the Victorian Points Test (VPT)?

      The Victorian Points Test is the ranking system Victoria uses to select Registrations of Interest (ROIs) for subclass 190 and subclass 491 state nomination. It scores seven factors to a theoretical maximum of 950 points. It is separate from the federal SkillSelect points test.

      What is the maximum Victorian Points Test score?

      Adding the maximum of each factor gives 950: age 300, English 200, Victorian salary 150, experience 100, qualification 100, skill level 50 and partner skills 50.

      What changed in the Victorian Points Test?

      Age is now scored for each year rather than in five-year bands, falling from 300 points at 29 to 16 points at 44. Victorian earnings are scored in 19 bands of $5,000, from 8 points at $80,000 to 150 points at $170,000 or more.

      What is the pass mark for the Victorian Points Test?

      There is no fixed pass mark. Victoria ranks Registrations of Interest against each other and invites higher-ranked candidates, so the score needed changes with demand.

      How many VPT points do I get for my age?

      Ages 20 to 29 score 300. Age 18 scores 265 and 19 scores 282. From 30 the points fall each year: 286, 270, 256, 240, 226, 210, 196, 180, 166, 150, 136, 106, 76, 46 and 16 at age 44.

      Does the VPT replace the federal 65-point test?

      No. You still need at least 65 points in the federal SkillSelect test to lodge an Expression of Interest. The VPT is only used by Victoria to rank candidates for nomination. Victorian nomination then adds 5 federal points for subclass 190 or 15 for subclass 491.

      How much does it cost to apply for Victorian nomination in 2026–27?

      Victoria announced a $60 (excluding GST) Registration of Interest fee and a $180 (excluding GST) nomination application fee for subclass 190 and 491 in 2026–27. The application fee is only payable if you are invited. GST applies if you are in Australia when you pay.

      Who gets the 50 partner points in the VPT?

      You get 50 points only if you claimed the 10 federal partner-skills points in your EOI, your partner is included in your application, and your partner’s occupation is on Victoria’s current priority occupation list.
