      What changed in the Victorian Points Test

      Victoria now scores Registrations of Interest for its 2026–27 skilled nomination program more finely. Two factors changed the most, so older calculators that use broad bands can be off by more than 60 points.

      Age points for each year

      Age is no longer scored in five-year bands. Ages 20–29 still get 300 points, but from 30 you lose 14 to 16 points each year down to 136 at 40. After that the drop is 30 points a year, reaching 16 at 44.

      Salary in $5,000 steps

      Victorian earnings now have 19 bands. Each $5,000 adds about 8 points, from 8 points at $80,000 up to the 150-point maximum at $170,000 or more.

      New fees for 2026–27

      Victoria announced a $60 + GST fee to lodge an ROI and a $180 + GST fee for a 190 or 491 nomination application. You pay the application fee only if you are invited.

      How the VPT score is calculated

      The Victorian Points Test adds up seven factors taken from your SkillSelect Expression of Interest and your Victorian Registration of Interest. The highest possible total is 950 points:

      Age (300) + English (200) + Victorian salary (150) + experience (100) + qualification (100) + ANZSCO skill level (50) + partner skills (50).

      Age and English together are worth more than half the total, which is why a 29-year-old with Superior English starts on 500 points before anything else is counted. For applicants who already live in Victoria, salary is the factor most within their control: moving up one $5,000 band is usually worth 7 or 8 points.

      The VPT does not have a pass mark. Victoria ranks all ROIs against each other and invites the highest-ranked candidates in each occupation group, so the score you need changes with demand. You still need at least 65 points in the federal SkillSelect points test to lodge your EOI.

      How to improve your Victorian Points Test score

      Retake your English test

      Moving from Competent to Proficient, or from Proficient to Superior, adds 100 points each time. No other single change is worth as much, so it is usually the best place to start.

      Lodge your ROI before your next birthday

      From age 30 every birthday costs points, and from 40 each birthday costs 30. If you are close to a birthday, lodging or updating your ROI sooner keeps the higher age score.

      Count all your Victorian earnings

      Use your full annual pre-tax salary from your current skilled job in Victoria, excluding superannuation. A pay rise that moves you into the next band adds about 8 points.

      Check partner eligibility

      The 50 partner points depend on Victoria’s current priority occupation list. If your partner’s occupation is on it and you already claim the federal 10 partner points, make sure they are included in your application.

      Victorian Points Test FAQ

      What is the Victorian Points Test (VPT)?

      The Victorian Points Test is the ranking system Victoria uses to select Registrations of Interest (ROIs) for subclass 190 and subclass 491 state nomination. It scores seven factors to a theoretical maximum of 950 points. It is separate from the federal SkillSelect points test.

      What is the maximum Victorian Points Test score?

      Adding the maximum of each factor gives 950: age 300, English 200, Victorian salary 150, experience 100, qualification 100, skill level 50 and partner skills 50.

      What changed in the Victorian Points Test?

      Age is now scored for each year rather than in five-year bands, falling from 300 points at 29 to 16 points at 44. Victorian earnings are scored in 19 bands of $5,000, from 8 points at $80,000 to 150 points at $170,000 or more.

      What is the pass mark for the Victorian Points Test?

      There is no fixed pass mark. Victoria ranks Registrations of Interest against each other and invites higher-ranked candidates, so the score needed changes with demand.

      How many VPT points do I get for my age?

      Ages 20 to 29 score 300. Age 18 scores 265 and 19 scores 282. From 30 the points fall each year: 286, 270, 256, 240, 226, 210, 196, 180, 166, 150, 136, 106, 76, 46 and 16 at age 44.

      Does the VPT replace the federal 65-point test?

      No. You still need at least 65 points in the federal SkillSelect test to lodge an Expression of Interest. The VPT is only used by Victoria to rank candidates for nomination. Victorian nomination then adds 5 federal points for subclass 190 or 15 for subclass 491.

      How much does it cost to apply for Victorian nomination in 2026–27?

      Victoria announced a $60 (excluding GST) Registration of Interest fee and a $180 (excluding GST) nomination application fee for subclass 190 and 491 in 2026–27. The application fee is only payable if you are invited. GST applies if you are in Australia when you pay.

      Who gets the 50 partner points in the VPT?

      You get 50 points only if you claimed the 10 federal partner-skills points in your EOI, your partner is included in your application, and your partner’s occupation is on Victoria’s current priority occupation list.
