      FAQ

      Questions about SkillSelect points

      What is the pass mark?

      65 points. That’s the minimum to lodge an EOI. Invitation cut-offs are usually much higher for popular occupations, so the pass mark is rarely enough on its own for subclass 189.

      How do nomination points work?

      Subclass 190 adds 5 points when a state or territory nominates you. Subclass 491 adds 15 points for state nomination or for sponsorship by an eligible relative in a designated regional area. You can include these points when you work out whether you reach 65.

      Why is my experience capped at 20?

      Overseas and Australian skilled employment together can’t add more than 20 points, however many years you have.

      Is this the same as the Victorian Points Test?

      No. This is the federal test every 189, 190 and 491 applicant needs. Victoria ranks its 190 and 491 candidates with its own test scored out of 950. You can calculate your VPT score too.
