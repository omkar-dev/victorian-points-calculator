Summary

Plastic and Reconstructive Surgeon (ANZSCO 253517) is on the MLTSSL and CSOL lists and can be used for subclass 189, 190, 491 skilled visas.

Skills are assessed by MedBA.

In the 18 December 2023 SkillSelect round, the lowest score invited for Plastic and Reconstructive Surgeon was 70 points. It wasn’t among the occupations invited in the latest round on 4 June 2026.

As at 30 September 2026, fewer than 20 EOIs were waiting for subclass 189, fewer than 20 for 190 and fewer than 20 for 491.

It belongs to the Surgeons group (2535), with a 2025-26 occupation ceiling of 500 places and 477 left for subclass 189 after 2024–25 grants.
