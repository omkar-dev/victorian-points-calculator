Summary

Civil Engineer (ANZSCO 233211) is on the MLTSSL and CSOL lists and can be used for subclass 189, 190, 491 skilled visas.

Skills are assessed by Engineers Australia.

In the 7 November 2024 SkillSelect round, the lowest score invited for Civil Engineer was 85 points. It wasn’t among the occupations invited in the latest round on 4 June 2026.

As at 30 September 2026, 8,777 EOIs were waiting for subclass 189, 15,837 for 190 and 16,465 for 491.

It belongs to the Civil Engineering Professionals group (2332), with a 2025-26 occupation ceiling of 745 places and none left for subclass 189 after 2024–25 grants.
