Summary

Orthopaedic Surgeon (ANZSCO 253514) is on the MLTSSL and CSOL lists and can be used for subclass 189, 190, 491 skilled visas.

Skills are assessed by MedBA.

In the 4 June 2026 SkillSelect round, the lowest score invited for Orthopaedic Surgeon was 85 points.

As at 30 September 2026, fewer than 20 EOIs were waiting for subclass 189, fewer than 20 for 190 and fewer than 20 for 491.

It belongs to the Surgeons group (2535), with a 2025-26 occupation ceiling of 500 places and 477 left for subclass 189 after 2024–25 grants.
