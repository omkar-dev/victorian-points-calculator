Summary

Chiropractor (ANZSCO 252111) is on the MLTSSL list and can be used for subclass 189, 190, 491 skilled visas.

Skills are assessed by CCEA.

In the 4 June 2026 SkillSelect round, the lowest score invited for Chiropractor was 80 points.

As at 30 September 2026, 134 EOIs were waiting for subclass 189, 73 for 190 and 23 for 491.

It belongs to the Chiropractors and Osteopaths group (2521), with a 2025-26 occupation ceiling of 500 places and <500 left for subclass 189 after 2024–25 grants.
