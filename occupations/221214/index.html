Summary

Internal Auditor (ANZSCO 221214) is on the MLTSSL and CSOL lists and can be used for subclass 189, 190, 491 skilled visas.

Skills are assessed by VETASSESS.

In the 7 November 2024 SkillSelect round, the lowest score invited for Internal Auditor was 90 points. It wasn’t among the occupations invited in the latest round on 4 June 2026.

As at 30 September 2026, 585 EOIs were waiting for subclass 189, 1,531 for 190 and 1,772 for 491.

It belongs to the Auditors, Company Secretaries and Corporate Treasurers group (2212), with a 2025-26 occupation ceiling of 500 places and none left for subclass 189 after 2024–25 grants.
