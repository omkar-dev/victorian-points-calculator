Summary

Motorcycle Mechanic (ANZSCO 321213) is on the MLTSSL and CSOL lists and can be used for subclass 189, 190, 491 skilled visas.

As at 30 September 2026, fewer than 20 EOIs were waiting for subclass 189, fewer than 20 for 190 and fewer than 20 for 491.
