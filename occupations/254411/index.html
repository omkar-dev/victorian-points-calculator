Summary

Nurse Practitioner (ANZSCO 254411) is on the MLTSSL and CSOL lists and can be used for subclass 189, 190, 491 skilled visas.

Skills are assessed by ANMAC.

In the 4 June 2026 SkillSelect round, the lowest score invited for Nurse Practitioner was 80 points.

As at 30 September 2026, fewer than 20 EOIs were waiting for subclass 189, fewer than 20 for 190 and fewer than 20 for 491.

It belongs to the Registered Nurses group (2544), with a 2025-26 occupation ceiling of 13,929 places and 10,390 left for subclass 189 after 2024–25 grants.
