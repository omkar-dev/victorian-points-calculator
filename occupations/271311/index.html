Summary

Solicitor (ANZSCO 271311) is on the MLTSSL and CSOL lists and can be used for subclass 189, 190, 491 skilled visas.

Skills are assessed by Legal admissions authority of a state or territory.

In the 4 June 2026 SkillSelect round, the lowest score invited for Solicitor was 80 points.

As at 30 September 2026, 739 EOIs were waiting for subclass 189, 637 for 190 and 320 for 491.

It belongs to the Solicitors group (2713), with a 2025-26 occupation ceiling of 943 places and 850 left for subclass 189 after 2024–25 grants.
