Summary

Quality Assurance Manager (ANZSCO 139916) is on the STSOL and CSOL lists and can be used for subclass 190, 491 skilled visas.

Skills are assessed by VETASSESS.

Quality Assurance Manager isn’t on the MLTSSL, so subclass 189 isn’t available. State or territory nomination (subclass 190 or 491) may be.

It belongs to the Other Specialist Managers group (1399), with a 2025-26 occupation ceiling of 670 places and 533 left for subclass 189 after 2024–25 grants.
