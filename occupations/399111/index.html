Summary

Boat Builder and Repairer (ANZSCO 399111) is on the MLTSSL and CSOL lists and can be used for subclass 189, 190, 491 skilled visas.

Skills are assessed by TRA.

In the 4 June 2026 SkillSelect round, the lowest score invited for Boat Builder and Repairer was 80 points.

As at 30 September 2026, 20 EOIs were waiting for subclass 189, fewer than 20 for 190 and fewer than 20 for 491.

It belongs to the Boat Builders and Shipwrights group (3991), with a 2025-26 occupation ceiling of 500 places and 483 left for subclass 189 after 2024–25 grants.
