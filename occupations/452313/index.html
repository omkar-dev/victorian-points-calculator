Summary

Horse Riding Coach or Instructor (ANZSCO 452313) is on the STSOL list and can be used for subclass 190, 491 skilled visas.

Skills are assessed by VETASSESS.

Horse Riding Coach or Instructor isn’t on the MLTSSL, so subclass 189 isn’t available. State or territory nomination (subclass 190 or 491) may be.

It belongs to the Sports Coaches, Instructors and Officials group (4523), with a 2025-26 occupation ceiling of 588 places and 522 left for subclass 189 after 2024–25 grants.
