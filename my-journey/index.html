About the tracker

What is the date of effect?

It’s the date your EOI points last changed: when you lodged it, or when a change such as a new English score or a birthday changed your points. For occupations with a tie at the cut-off, SkillSelect invites earlier dates of effect first.

Where is my data stored?

Only in this browser’s local storage. Clearing site data or using a private window removes it, so export a backup if it matters to you.

Does my EOI expire?

An EOI stays in SkillSelect for two years. The tracker shows when that will happen so you can lodge a new one in time.
