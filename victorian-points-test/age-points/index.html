VPT age points for every year

Age is the biggest factor in the Victorian Points Test, worth up to 300 of the 950 points. For 2026–27 Victoria scores age year by year instead of in five-year bands, so every birthday after 29 changes your score.

What the table means

Everyone aged 20 to 29 gets the full 300 points, so there’s no advantage to being 22 rather than 29. Ages 18 and 19 score a little less: 265 and 282.

From 30 the score drops each year: 286 at 30, 210 at 35 and 136 at 40. After 40 the steps get bigger, 30 points a year, falling to 16 at 44. At 45 the score is 0.

Use your age on the day you lodge or update your Registration of Interest. If a birthday is close, lodging before it keeps the higher score.

Questions about VPT age points

How many VPT points do I get for my age?

Ages 20 to 29 score the maximum 300 points. Age 18 scores 265 and 19 scores 282. From 30 the points fall every year, to 136 at 40 and 16 at 44.

How many points do I lose at each birthday?

From 31 to 40 each birthday costs 14 or 16 points. From 41 to 44 each one costs 30, so lodging before a birthday matters most in your forties.

Do I get VPT age points at 45?

No. Age 45 scores 0, and you also need to be under 45 for the federal subclass 190 and 491 points test.
