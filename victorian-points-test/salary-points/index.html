VPT salary points by earnings band

Victorian salary is worth up to 150 of the 950 points. For 2026–27 it’s scored in 19 bands of $5,000, so even a modest pay rise can lift your score.

What the table means

Points start at $80,000 with 8 points and rise by 7 or 8 every $5,000, reaching the maximum 150 at $170,000. Anything above that still scores 150.

Only current skilled employment in Victoria counts. If you’re offshore or earn under $80,000, this factor is 0, and age, English and experience carry your score.

Questions about VPT salary points

How many VPT points do I get for my salary?

Victorian earnings score from 8 points at $80,000 to the full 150 at $170,000 or more, in 19 bands of $5,000. Under $80,000 scores 0.

What salary counts for the Victorian Points Test?

Your yearly salary before tax, without super, from your current skilled employment in Victoria. If you work outside Victoria, salary scores 0.

How much is each $5,000 band worth?

Each band adds 7 or 8 points, so a pay rise that moves you up one band adds about 8 points.
