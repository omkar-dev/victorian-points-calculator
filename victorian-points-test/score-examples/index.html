What the examples show

The highest score here, 780 for the senior specialist, comes from Superior English, long experience and partner points. The lowest, 420 for the recent graduate, offshore, has Competent English and no Victorian salary.

Age and English make up half of the 950 points. Salary only counts for skilled work in Victoria, so offshore applicants rely on English and experience.

Questions about VPT scores

What is a good Victorian Points Test score?

There is no pass mark. Victoria ranks Registrations of Interest against each other and invites the highest scorers in each occupation group, so a good score depends on who else is in the pool for your occupation.

What is the highest possible VPT score?

950: age 300, English 200, Victorian salary 150, experience 100, qualification 100, skill level 50 and partner 50.

Which change raises a VPT score the most?

English. Moving up one level, from Competent to Proficient or from Proficient to Superior, adds 100 points, more than any other single change.
