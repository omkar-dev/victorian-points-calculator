How this works

Where do the cut-offs come from?

Home Affairs publishes the minimum score invited for each occupation after every round. We use the official tables from July 2023 onwards. Cut-offs depend on how many places each round has, so the next round can be higher or lower.

What happens at the cut-off score?

When there are more EOIs at the cut-off score than places, SkillSelect invites the earliest dates of effect first. Home Affairs now publishes one tie-break date per round, not one per occupation, so we can’t say exactly where you’d fall.

Why are EOI numbers so high?

The EOI pool counts EOIs, not people. Many people lodge several EOIs or select several visas, and some EOIs are never withdrawn. Treat the pool as a sense of competition, not a queue.
