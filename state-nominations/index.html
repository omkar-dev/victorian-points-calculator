    How each state selects

    Every state runs its own process. Always check the official page before you apply.

    VictoriaVIC

    You need a SkillSelect EOI and a Victorian Registration of Interest. Victoria ranks ROIs with the Victorian Points Test (no pass mark) and invites the highest-ranked, including within priority industries. From 2026–27 there is a $60 ROI fee and a $180 nomination fee (excluding GST). The 2026–27 program had not opened as at 15 October 2026.

    Official VIC page →

    New South WalesNSW

    NSW invites candidates for subclass 190 from SkillSelect EOIs in occupations on the NSW Skills List, and you cannot apply directly. For subclass 491 there are three pathways: apply directly if employed by a regional NSW employer, be invited by Investment NSW, or apply as a recent regional NSW graduate. Both visas are closed until new places are allocated, and NSW is reviewing its settings for the next program year.

    Official NSW page →

    QueenslandQLD

    Migration Queensland takes Registrations of Interest under set streams: skilled workers living in Queensland, offshore workers on the offshore list, building and construction workers, Queensland university graduates, and small business owners in regional Queensland (491). 2025–26 ROIs are closed with all places filled. Queensland is awaiting Commonwealth allocations before publishing its 2026–27 settings.

    Official QLD page →

    South AustraliaSA

    South Australia nominates under four streams: SA Graduates, Skilled Employment in SA, Outer Regional Skilled Employment, and Offshore. Onshore candidates lodge a free ROI through the Apply Portal. Offshore candidates are selected from SkillSelect EOIs. Nomination applications cost $394. 2025–26 ROIs closed on 2 June 2026, and no 2026–27 program has been announced yet.

    Official SA page →

    Western AustraliaWA

    WA runs invitation rounds from SkillSelect EOIs under a General stream (WASMOL Schedule 1 and 2) and a Graduate stream, ranked by EOI points and favouring priority sectors such as building and construction, healthcare and social assistance, hospitality and tourism, and education. Candidates eligible for both visas are generally invited for subclass 491 first. The last round listed was on 20 May 2026.

    Official WA page →

    TasmaniaTAS

    Candidates register through the TAS Skilled Migration Gateway, which ranks eligible candidates automatically. 'Gold pass' attributes can lead straight to a nomination application. The 2026–27 program opened to ROIs on 17 August 2026 with 1,250 places for subclass 190 and 800 for subclass 491. Invitations are issued progressively from 20 August, and the 491 Overseas Skilled Occupation Profile pathway will not issue invitations this year.

    Official TAS page →

    Australian Capital TerritoryACT

    Candidates submit a points-based Canberra Matrix, and the highest-ranked Matrix in each occupation are invited in invitation rounds that use a pro-rata share of the allocation. The first 2026–27 round was the week starting 12 October 2026 (subclass 190 only). The ACT says its 2026–27 allocation is not yet confirmed.

    Official ACT page →

    Northern TerritoryNT

    The NT Government takes nomination applications directly through its portal, mainly from NT residents and NT graduates, plus selected offshore streams. Current 2026–27 details could not be verified because the official site blocked automated access, so check the page for the latest status.

    Official NT page →
